Hello and welcome to the Manamon 2 Item Guide. This is a (mostly) complete listing of all items found in Manamon 2, organised by location. Of course, we are only human and have no doubt missed an item or two. If you found something that's not in the guide, feel free to contact me and I will endeavour to update the guide as soon as possible. Contact info is at the bottom.
Now, with that out of the way, let's get on with the show.
Note: A star (*) denotes a hidden item, and a @ sign (@) denotes an area.
